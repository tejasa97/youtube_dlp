Synthetic deterministic UMP payloads for bounded SABR_REDIRECT,
SABR_CONTEXT_UPDATE, SABR_CONTEXT_SENDING_POLICY, SABR_ERROR, and
RELOAD_PLAYER_RESPONSE coverage.

These fixtures are retained for regression maintenance of an experimental
extension. SABR is excluded from project parity targets and roadmap
deliverables. No live network capture. No claim of full SABR client parity.

## Files

- `redirect-valid.ump.bin` — single SABR_REDIRECT to a trusted googlevideo HTTPS URL
- `context-update-active.ump.bin` — SABR_CONTEXT_UPDATE with send_by_default
- `sending-policy-packed.ump.bin` — packed start/stop/discard policy
- `mixed-media-redirect.ump.bin` — selected-track media plus one redirect

Generated by `internal/protocol/youtubeump` test helpers. `TestDirectiveFixturesByteIdenticalAndSynthetic` regenerates every fixture and asserts byte-identical equality, and scans for secret/live-capture markers.

## Unsupported variants (documented)

- `STREAM_PROTECTION_STATUS` (58) and live metadata (31) remain `ErrUnsupportedDirective`
- davidzeng0 alternate `ReloadPlayerResponse` unnamed nesting
- SABR_ERROR code-specific recovery beyond generic retry
- Empty/partial `SabrError` (missing type or code)
