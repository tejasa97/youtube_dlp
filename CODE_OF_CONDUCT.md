# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want `ytdlp-go` to be a technically rigorous, welcoming, and safe project
for contributors and users of every background and experience level.

Participation in this project means treating other people with respect,
assuming good faith while evidence is incomplete, and keeping disagreement
focused on the work rather than the person.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate clearly, patiently, and constructively.
- Critique ideas, implementations, evidence, and trade-offs—not people.
- Respect different levels of experience and invite useful questions.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and help repair their effects.
- Protect credentials, personal information, private media, and other sensitive
  data encountered during debugging or review.
- Follow project security, fixture-provenance, licensing, and responsible-use
  requirements.
- Stop an interaction when a maintainer asks for de-escalation or a change of
  venue.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory, sexualized, demeaning, or personally insulting language or
  imagery.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, private media, sensitive diagnostics, or
  unsafe proof-of-concept material.
- Bad-faith issue flooding, review manipulation, impersonation, or retaliation.
- Using project spaces to promote unlawful access, DRM circumvention, or
  infringement.
- Continuing conduct that a maintainer has already identified as harmful or
  disruptive.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release and support channels, and other spaces where someone is representing
the project. It also applies to private interactions when those interactions
materially affect participation in the project.

## Reporting

Do not litigate a sensitive conduct report in a public issue. Submit a private
report through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/tejasa97/ytdlp-go/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `[conduct]`.

Include the relevant links, dates, participants, and a concise description of
what occurred. Do not include unrelated personal information. Reports will be
reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical, subject to safety and
legal obligations.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request a change in behavior, pause an
interaction, restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. The
response will be proportionate to the behavior, its impact, prior warnings,
and the safety of the community.

Maintainers who are directly involved in a report should recuse themselves
when another qualified reviewer is available. Retaliation against a reporter,
witness, or reviewer is itself a violation.

## Project boundaries

This code does not replace the [security policy](SECURITY.md),
[support policy](SUPPORT.md), contribution requirements, or applicable law.
Those documents continue to govern their respective technical and legal
boundaries.
